# Contributing

Thank you for reading closely enough to want to help. This book stays useful only if its claims stay accurate, so corrections and new evidence are the most valuable contributions.

## What helps most

- **Corrections.** A number that does not match its source, a broken link, a typo, an unclear translation. Use the *Correction* issue form.
- **New evidence.** A study or a test that supports, contradicts or limits a claim in the book. Use the *New evidence or counterexample* form and describe how the result was measured. Your own tests are welcome; they are labelled as practitioner reports, never as proof.
- **New research papers.** If a relevant paper is missing, the evidence form works for that too.

## What we do not accept

- **Advice for a specific website.** The book explains principles and evidence; it cannot audit individual sites. Questions belong in [Discussions](https://github.com/ivantsai1982/geo-lab/discussions).
- **Mentions of tools, vendors or services.** The book does not name commercial providers, so that no one can buy their way into it.
- **Translations or adapted versions.** The content license (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0) does not allow distributing modified versions, including translations. The author makes every language edition so that each one is checked against the source. You can request a language with the *Language request* form.

## Pull requests

Small fixes (typos, links, formatting) can go straight to a pull request. For anything that changes a claim, open an issue first so the evidence can be discussed. By submitting a pull request you agree that your contribution may be published under the repository's licenses (content: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; code: MIT).

## How changes are released

Every change gets a version number and a [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) entry. All editions share one version.

# 參與方式

如果你讀得仔細到想幫忙，謝謝你。這本書的價值在於結論準確，所以修正和新證據是最有用的貢獻。

- **指正錯誤：** 數字和出處對不上、連結失效、錯字、翻譯不通順。請用「Correction」表單。
- **新證據：** 支持、反駁或補充書中某個結論的研究或測試。請用「New evidence or counterexample」表單，並說明怎麼測的。你自己的實測也歡迎，會標為從業者的實測報告，不會當作證明。
- **不接受：** 針對個別網站的諮詢（請到 Discussions）、要求書中提到某個工具或服務商、翻譯或改編版本（內容授權 CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 不允許散布改作；各語言版本都由作者製作並核對）。
- **Pull request：** 錯字、連結、格式可以直接提；會改變結論的內容，請先開 issue 討論。提交即表示同意以本倉庫的授權發布。
